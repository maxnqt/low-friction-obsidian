## Weekly reset
 
Ten minutes, Friday or Sunday. Not optional, but also not sacred.
 
- [ ] Open **Everything open**. Delete anything you're not going to do. Deleting is the point of this exercise, not triaging.
- [ ] Pick 3–5 things → tag `#next`. Untag last week's leftovers if they no longer matter.
- [ ] Skim this week's dailies **once**, at speed. Anything that appeared 3+ times → open the relevant [[Playbook]] and add a line.
- [ ] Every active project has a filled-in **Next action**.
- [ ] Close the parking lot without acting on it.
